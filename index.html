About

Hi there! I’m Mustafa. I’m currently studying Maths and Data Science, and I love how much variety there is in what I’m learning. I enjoy stats and pure theory just as much as machine learning and actually building things with data.

I still haven’t narrowed down exactly what I want to focus on. That used to bother me, but I’ve realised I quite like being undecided for now. It means I can enjoy different parts of the subject without feeling like I need to choose one too early.

What I care about most is understanding why something works. I’d rather not run some code or use a model if I don’t properly understand what’s going on underneath it. I tend to slow things down, understand each part properly, and then build everything back up myself. It takes longer, but I enjoy learning that way.

I’m still a student, so at the moment that mostly means coursework, side projects, and getting my hands on real data whenever I can. I’m also looking for internships whenever the opportunity comes up, partly so I can get more experience working on real problems and figure out which areas of research interest me most.
